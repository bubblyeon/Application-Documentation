# Application-Documentation
My app would help students understand how their daily habits affect their focus,
memory, and learning. I would design it for college students who struggle to stay focused or find
effective study routines. As for the functionality, users would log their sleep, study sessions,
distractions, etc. to find patterns and they can read articles on there based on basic neuroscience
research to help. The app would hopefully help students reduce unproductive study time through
better habits.
