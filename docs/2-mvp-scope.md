# MVP Scope & Feature Backlog 

## The Ultimate Product Vision

This app will help people understand how their daily habits affect their studying, sleep, focus, and overall productivity.
The app will use neuroscience research to help develop effective routines that students can track using the app :)

## The Launch Goal (v1.0 MVP)

To launch a functional version by July 2027 focusing strictly on core value.

## Feature Prioritization Matrix

*   **Must Have (MVP Lineup):**

    *   `Daily Habit Logging`: Students log sleep hours, study sessions, and distractions each day

    *   `POMODORO timer`: A timer set to a default 25 minutes working/5 minutes break for study sessions

    *   `Pattern Insights`: Simple charts and summaries showing how sleep, distractions, and study time relate to productivity

*   **Nice to Have (Post-Launch Backlog):**

    *   `Study Routine Recommendations`: Suggests personalized study routines based on the student's logged patterns. It can wait because the app will need more data before it can generate them.

    *   `Neuroscience Article Library`: Articles on neuroscience research related to the app's features and linked to the user's insights. This can also wait since the user can do their own research for the time being.
